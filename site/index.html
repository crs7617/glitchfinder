Glitchfinder

Glitch tokens are vocabulary entries that a model barely saw during training. Their embeddings are degenerate, and forcing the model to use them produces bizarre output. This tool detects them from embedding matrices alone.

Method & Limits

Tied models (shared input/output embeddings): we measure cosine distance from a reference centroid of known-unused tokens. Tokens close to this "garbage direction" are suspicious.

Untied models: weight decay pushes unused input embeddings toward zero. We rank by L2 norm — lower norm = more suspicious.

Verification: for models ≤1B, we test candidates with 3 prompt templates. If max probability < 1% across all prompts, the token is confirmed as a glitch. Larger models are indicator-only ("candidates, unverified").

Limits

  • Safetensors only. Models with only .bin weights are not supported.
  • CPU, 8 GB RAM. Very large embedding matrices may not fit.
  • Verification limited to ≤1B parameter models.
  • Indicators are heuristics, not guarantees. Low-norm tokens may be legitimate but rare. Cosine proximity to the garbage cluster may be coincidental.
  • 3 prompt templates cannot cover all possible contexts where a token might be predictable.
